Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

✓ GDPR-aligned — We never sell your data

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to deliver our services: contact details (name, email), project files shared by the client, and billing information for invoicing purposes.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To communicate about your project
  • To deliver and invoice for services rendered
  • To improve our internal workflows — no personal data is shared externally

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to any third party. Your data is never used for advertising or shared with external analytics platforms.

4. Data Retention

Project files are retained for up to 30 days after delivery, then permanently deleted unless the client requests extended storage in writing.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us directly.

6. Contact

For any data-related requests, please reach out at: contact@amiravision.com
